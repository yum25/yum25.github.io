Something I recently came across while revamping this personal site of mine was dealing with assets (most commonly, images). After working on my own site, I've noticed that within a lot of portfolio sites, assets are treated as an afterthought. This was definitely the case for me - it's easy to just take a screenshot of something as an image preview, throw it into your assets folder, and focus on the more involved aspects of coding up your website.

Recently though, I've been thinking more about bundle sizes ever since reading an article about why your website should be under 14kB in size. If you're not careful, your images can overexceed that by tens to hundreds of times, and often it doesn't have to be that way.

Sometimes while updating my site I like to look through other dev portfolios from people at a similar stage as me in their careers for inspiration. One website I came across loaded ~40 MB upon the first page load and took 15+ seconds to load the main portfolio page. Other portfolios had decent page load times, but still served images in the MB range (likely a high resolution image they got from their phone). I myself made these mistakes as well.
